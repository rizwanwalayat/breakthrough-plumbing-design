Our Story

Built on honesty.
Proven by results.

Breakthrough Mechanical Inc. was founded with one simple belief: plumbing work should be done right, explained clearly, and priced honestly. No runaround. No hidden charges. Just great trades work from people who genuinely care.

What started as a small residential service operation in Edmonton has grown into a full-service plumbing and mechanical company trusted by homeowners, property developers, and commercial contractors across Alberta.

We are committed to open communication, working closely with our clients every step of the way — because we know that behind every call is a home or a business that needs to run smoothly.
